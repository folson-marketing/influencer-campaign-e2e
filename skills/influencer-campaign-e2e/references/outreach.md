# Outreach

## Rules for every email

- **Personalize:** mention one specific piece of the creator's content, say why it fits the product, state the deal clearly, and give one simple next step (reply, fill a form, book a call).
- **Keep it short:** 80–150 words for the first email; subject under 50 characters, no all-caps or excessive punctuation.
- **Be honest:** don't invent numbers or promise fees or perks the user hasn't approved.
- **Opt-out:** end with an opt-out line (e.g. "If this isn't a fit, just let me know and I won't follow up."), for CASL / CAN-SPAM compliance.

## First email — English

```
Subject: {Brand} x {Creator first name} — {one-line hook}

Hi {Name},

I loved your {specific video/post} about {topic} — {one genuine detail}.

I'm {Sender} from {Brand}. We make {one-line product}. Your audience {why fit}.

We'd love to {deal: send you {product} / a paid {deliverable} for {fee range or "your rate"}}.

Would you be open to it? Happy to share details.

{Signature}
P.S. If it's not a fit, no worries — just let me know.
```

## DM version (no email address)

Same structure compressed to 300 characters or fewer: the specific content hook, one line on the product, the ask, and the opt-out. The user sends it manually on the platform.

## Follow-ups

- **Follow-up 1** (+4 days): short, friendly nudge in the same thread.
- **Follow-up 2** (+5 days more, last one): light tone, make it easy to say no: "no worries if the timing isn't right."
- Always draft in the original thread; never start a new one.
