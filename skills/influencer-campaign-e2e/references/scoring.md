# Scoring rubric

Score each candidate 0–100 and record the reasoning next to the score.

| Dimension | Weight | What to look at |
|---|---|---|
| Audience fit | 25 | Region, language, age / gender match the target |
| Content fit | 20 | Niche, tone, whether the product fits naturally on screen |
| Engagement quality | 20 | ER vs. the tier benchmark; whether comments look genuine |
| Consistency | 15 | Median recent views ÷ followers; whether views swing wildly |
| Activity | 10 | Posting frequency over the last 30 days |
| Brand safety & commercial | 10 | Not over-saturated with ads, no controversies, has worked with similar brands |

## Engagement-rate benchmarks

Rules of thumb, not hard thresholds. Adjust by platform and tier.

| Tier | Followers | Typical ER |
|---|---|---|
| Nano | 1k–10k | > 4% |
| Micro | 10k–100k | 2–4% |
| Mid | 100k–500k | 1.5–3% |
| Macro | 500k+ | 1–2% |

## Fake-follower and risk signals

Deduct points and explain in the notes when you see any of these:
- Many followers but very low views
- Comments that are mostly emojis or generic praise
- Sudden spikes in follower count
- Audience region doesn't match the content language
- Recent controversial content

## Tiers

- **A**: 75 and above
- **B**: 60–74
- **C**: below 60 (not contacted by default)
